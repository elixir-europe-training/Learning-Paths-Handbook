## What is this step about?
Identifying the DOMAIN is the starting point to set the context, content, and structure of the learning path.

## What is a DOMAIN?
!!! info "A DOMAIN is ..."

    Here you can put a note using admonitions.

    A DOMAIN is the overarching field or discipline that frames the Learning Path. It defines the scope of knowledge, practices, and skills that the pathway will address and helps align content with recognised bodies of knowledge.


## Why do we need to identify a DOMAIN?
Identifying the DOMAIN is essential from several perspectives:
##### From an educator’s perspective: 
It provides a clear framework/reference to guide subsequent decisions (next steps) in developing the learning path.
##### From a learner’s perspective: 
A clearly stated DOMAIN ensures clarity, helping learners understand the focus and goals of the learning path.

## How do we identify a DOMAIN?

Consider the organisational or disciplinary context in which the Learning Path will be applied.
Review existing programmes, curricula, and strategic priorities to determine the most relevant scope.
Confirm the DOMAIN with subject-matter experts to ensure accuracy and shared understanding.

## Use-case example
**Bioinformatics for Genomics Learning Path:** The DOMAIN is genomics-focused bioinformatics, establishing the boundaries for topics, resources, and skills to be included.
