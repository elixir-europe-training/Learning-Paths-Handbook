## What is this step about?
- Clarifies how TOPICS connect based on required prior knowledge and skills.
- Ensures learners have the necessary preparation before progressing through the Learning Path.
- Identifies potential gaps that may need bridging materials or support.

## What are PREREQUISITES?
Prerequisites are the knowledge, skills, or experiences learners must possess before engaging with a specific TOPIC or STAGE within the Learning Path.
 
## Why do we need to connect TOPICS based on PREREQUISITES?
- To promote a coherent learning experience where each TOPIC builds on the previous one
- To reduce learner frustration by ensuring adequate preparation for advanced content
- To highlight where supplementary resources may be needed to close gaps

## How do we connect TOPICS based on PREREQUISITES?
+- Map dependencies between TOPICS and STAGES to define a logical sequence.
+- Specify the knowledge or skills required before beginning each TOPIC.
+- Provide optional preparatory materials for learners who need to meet the prerequisites.
+- Review the sequence with subject-matter experts to validate assumptions and adjust ordering as needed.
 
 ## Use-case example
+- **Bioinformatics for Genomics Learning Path:** Proficiency with command-line tools and basic statistics is a prerequisite before undertaking TOPICS such as variant calling or machine learning methods for genomics.
