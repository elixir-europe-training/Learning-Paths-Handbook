## What is this step about?
This step defines and documents the LEARNING OUTCOMES that articulate what learners will know or be able to do upon completing the Learning Path.


## What are LEARNING OUTCOMES?
LEARNING OUTCOMES are concise, measurable statements that describe the knowledge, skills, or behaviours learners should demonstrate after engaging with the Learning Path.
 
## Why do we need to annotate LEARNING OUTCOMES of the learning path?
- To align content, activities, and assessments with the intended results for learners
- To communicate expectations clearly to stakeholders and participants
- To evaluate whether the Learning Path achieves its goals

## Why do we need this step
Defining Learning Outcomes ensures the Learning Path remains purposeful and that each component contributes to the intended learner achievements.
 
## How do we annotate LEARNING OUTCOMES of the learning path?
- Use action-oriented verbs (e.g. apply, analyse, evaluate) that align with the expected level of complexity.
- Ensure each outcome is specific and measurable.
- Map outcomes to TOPICS, STAGES, and assessments to verify alignment.
- Review outcomes with stakeholders to confirm relevance and clarity.

## Use-case example

 **Bioinformatics for Genomics Learning Path:** Learners will be able to design and execute a variant calling pipeline, interpret quality metrics, and communicate findings in a format suitable for collaborative research.
