Follow-up training helps learners consolidate knowledge and continue developing skills after completing a Learning Path. Consider the following approaches:

- Offer refresher sessions or clinics to revisit complex concepts and address questions that arise during practice.
- Provide curated resources, such as readings, tool demonstrations, or recorded webinars, to deepen understanding.
- Encourage peer-to-peer activities—study groups, discussion forums, or code reviews—to sustain engagement.
- Align follow-up activities with the Learning Outcomes to ensure progress is measurable and relevant.
